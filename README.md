# Windblown

Wind for Minecraft. Grass, flowers, crops, leaves, vines and kelp sway in a breeze that rolls across the land in gusts,
and plants rustle when you, other players or mobs walk through them. No shader pack needed.

Windblown is a small client-side mod for Minecraft on Fabric, NeoForge and Forge, compatible with Mod Menu on Fabric.

Supported versions: Fabric 1.18.2 and 1.19 to 26.3, NeoForge 1.21 to 26.3, Forge 1.18.2, 1.19.2 and 1.20.1.
Works with Sodium and Embeddium.

## Features

- **Plants sway in the wind** - grass, ferns, flowers, saplings, crops, tall plants, sugar cane and bamboo bend with
  the wind, rooted at the ground so nothing floats.
- **Leaves and vines too** - tree leaves shiver and drift, vines and hanging roots swing, seagrass and kelp drift in a
  slow current under water.
- **Gusts you can see** - the wind comes in waves that roll across a field in one direction, which slowly turns over
  time.
- **Weather** - rain makes the wind pick up and thunderstorms blow harder still. Plants under a roof or deep in a cave
  barely move.
- **Rustling** - walking, running or riding through grass, crops and leaves makes them rustle, and so do mobs.
- **Adjustable** - wind strength from 10% to 300%, each kind of plant can be turned off on its own, and the rustling
  has its own volume.
- **Built to stay fast** - the motion runs in the terrain shader, so chunks are not rebuilt while the wind blows and
  there is no measurable frame rate cost.
- **Client-side only** - nothing to install on a server.

## Install

**Fabric**

1. Install [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/) for your version of Minecraft.
2. Put the Fabric Windblown jar for your version in your `mods` folder. Fabric API is not needed.
3. Start the game.

Optional: add [Mod Menu](https://modrinth.com/mod/modmenu) to get a settings screen (*Mods > Windblown > Configure*).

**NeoForge**

1. Install NeoForge for your version of Minecraft.
2. Put the NeoForge Windblown jar in your `mods` folder.
3. Start the game. Settings are under *Mods > Windblown > Config*.

**Forge (1.18.2, 1.19.2, 1.20.1)**

1. Install Forge for your version of Minecraft.
2. Put the Forge Windblown jar in your `mods` folder.
3. Start the game. Settings are under *Mods > Windblown > Config*.

## Notes

- With an Iris shader pack turned on, the shader pack draws the world and Windblown's plant motion pauses until you turn
  it off again. Rustling keeps working.
- Works together with Firelight.

## Building

`./gradlew build -Pminecraft_version=26.3` builds the Fabric jar; add `-Ploader=neoforge` or `-Ploader=forge` for the
other loaders (version configs in `versions/`, generated by `docs/gen_versions.py`). `./gradlew runSelftest` launches the game and runs the in-world checks.
